# Practice Hub

Track your daily habits, problem sets, and reflections here. Consistency matters more than intensity.

## Daily Routine Checklist
- [ ] Warm-up: Re-implement a recently learned structure/algorithm from memory.
- [ ] Timed problem (30-45 minutes) from patterns relevant to your current focus.
- [ ] Retrospective: Record insights, mistakes, and follow-up tasks.

## Weekly Goals
- Complete at least one mock interview or timed contest.
- Review flagged problems and solve them again without notes.
- Update pattern summaries with new takeaways.

## Components
- `patterns/` – Reusable strategies, templates, and notes categorized by topic.
- `challenges/` – Logs for problems solved, difficulty ratings, and reflections.

Keep this directory evolving as you identify what works best for you.
