# Resources

Curate your own list of references and keep it focused. Quality beats quantity—aim for materials you will actually use.

## Books
- *Introduction to Algorithms* (CLRS) – Comprehensive reference; use for deep dives.
- *Grokking Algorithms* – Visual explanations for quick refreshers.
- *Algorithm Design Manual* – Real-world heuristics and problem catalog.

## Courses & Playlists
- MIT OCW 6.006 and 6.046 – Rigorous lecture series with assignments.
- Princeton Algorithms (Coursera) – Java-focused with excellent visuals.
- NeetCode YouTube playlists – Interview-oriented walkthroughs.

## Problem Banks
- LeetCode (curate lists by topic and difficulty)
- HackerRank (for warm-ups and language-specific practice)
- Codeforces (timed contests, tougher problems)

## Tools
- VisuAlgo (interactive data structure visualizations)
- Big-O Cheat Sheets for quick complexity lookups
- Anki or Obsidian for spaced repetition notes

## Communities
- r/learnprogramming and r/cscareerquestions (discussion and mentorship)
- Discord servers or study groups with accountability buddies
- Local meetups or online cohorts focused on algorithm practice

Refresh this list every few months—retire sources you no longer need and highlight new favorites.
